Features

Simple to use.
Powerful underneath.

Everything just works, right out of the box.

One-click copy

Click any code to copy it instantly. Paste it where you need it. The clipboard auto-clears for safety.

Scan from screen

See a QR code on a website? Just click "Scan Screen" and the app reads it for you automatically.

Easy switching

Already using Google Authenticator? Import all your accounts in seconds. No re-scanning needed.
